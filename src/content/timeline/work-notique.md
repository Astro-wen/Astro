Co-founded an **AI recording pen and companion SaaS** for U.S. real estate professionals, addressing missed client details and repetitive CRM entry. Launched the pen on **Amazon**; used **20+ user interviews**, feedback, and MySQL / Tableau analysis to prioritize onboarding, adoption, and retention.

Built the SaaS **project-memory** experience with an AI workflow, using React, TypeScript, server APIs, and Cloudflare D1/R2; turned recordings, transcripts, and photos into reviewable AI briefs with citations and timestamps. Added review and change timelines; automated development enabled multiple iterations per day.

Defined **two-stage extraction and verification** with user approval before saving facts; added resumable AI jobs and offline evaluation for missed facts, unsupported citations, and consistency across conversations.
