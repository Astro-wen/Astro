If you're seeing this page, thank you!! Seriously, thank you so much ![](/media/about/thanks.gif)

I'm a product manager who grew up wild in data. Thanks to my STEM background, I can talk easily with engineering teams, and I understand and respect how complex the business really is. When it comes to whether a feature is any good, I don't put much trust in opinions. I'd rather run a test and watch how the data and metrics move. Games are the area I'm most interested in. I'm drawn to game design, combat design and balance design, and I also build in Godot and Unity.

I like studying what people need. To me, the fundamentals of a product manager come down to three things: empathy for people, a feel for data, and technical ability. I also firmly believe product managers aren't going to die out in this [crazy era](https://claude.com/blog/product-management-on-the-ai-exponential).

On the school side, I finished two majors in two years, [Statistics & Data Science](https://statistics.ucla.edu/) and [Business Economics](https://economics.ucla.edu/), and now I'm working on [Linguistics & Computer Science](https://linguistics.ucla.edu/).
